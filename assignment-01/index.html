My top 5 power rankings of albums that molded my taste in music.

  1. Diary of a Mad Band - Jodeci

    Devante Swing's signature chords and unique arrangements make this album a standout. I believe it has had a profound impact on 90s R&B and hip-hop.

  2. Purpose by Design - Fred Hammond & Radical for Christ

    This album probably created the blueprint for the modern gospel music sound. The arrangements, and live performances bridge contemporary and traditional styles seamlessly.

  3. Songs in the Key of Life - Stevie Wonder

    Although Overjoyed is my favorite track of all time from Stevie Wonder, his playing of the piano on Songs in the Key of Life album, and his songwriting skills display a influential blend of soul, funk, and jazz that has left a lasting impact on music.

  4. Guy - Guy

    Guy's self-titled album showcases a timeless blend between classic R&B and New Jack Swing, making it a classic in the R&B genre.

  5. Thriller - Michael Jackson

    Michael Jackson's Thriller album is not only a landmark in pop music, but also influential in shaping how modern artists approach music videos and lyrics that paint vivid narratives.
